# SourceTradie — Replit Build Brief

Build a polished, mobile-first web application called **SourceTradie** for Melbourne, Australia.

SourceTradie is **not a tradie directory**. It is a customer-first local trade dispatch platform.

The core idea is:

A homeowner describes what is wrong, SourceTradie qualifies the job, identifies the correct trade, captures location and timing, and then routes the request to an appropriate local tradie who can accept or decline the job.

Initial trades:

* Electricians
* Plumbers
* Heating & Cooling / HVAC

Initial market:

* Melbourne, Australia

## Brand

Brand name: **SourceTradie**

Primary headline:
**Something broken? SourceTradie it.**

Supporting copy:
**Tell us what’s wrong and we’ll help source the right local professional.**

Primary CTA:
**Source a Tradie**

Secondary CTA:
**Join as a Trade Partner**

The visual style should feel like a premium modern Australian technology startup — simple, trustworthy, clean, fast and highly polished.

Avoid:

* Generic tradie-template styling
* Construction clichés
* Excessive hard-hat/tool imagery
* Fake testimonials
* Fake user counts
* Fake tradie counts
* Fake response times

## Customer Job Flow

Build a beautiful multi-step conversational intake.

Ask:

1. What’s going on?
2. Do you know which service you need?

   * Plumbing
   * Electrical
   * Heating & Cooling
   * Not sure
3. Ask relevant follow-up questions based on the problem.
4. Suburb/postcode
5. Urgency

   * Emergency
   * Today
   * Next few days
   * Flexible
6. Preferred attendance time
7. Optional photo upload
8. Customer name
9. Phone
10. Email
11. Final job summary
12. Submit request

After submission show:

**Request received**

Then show an elegant status screen:

* Job understood
* Trade identified
* Location confirmed
* Availability requested
* Sourcing suitable professionals

Generate a readable job reference such as:
**ST-1042**

Do NOT pretend a tradie has been matched if no real match exists.

## Safety Logic

Do not diagnose dangerous electrical, gas or plumbing situations.

If the user mentions things such as:

* Smell of gas
* Sparks
* Electrical fire
* Live exposed wiring
* Major flooding
* Immediate danger

Show a safety warning and recommend appropriate emergency action rather than continuing ordinary booking flow.

## UX Requirements

Prioritise mobile.

Use:

* Large tap targets
* Excellent typography
* Simple transitions
* Clear progress indicators
* Card-based layouts
* Fast perceived performance

The homeowner experience should require almost no explanation.

The interface should feel more like a modern consumer app than a marketplace directory.

## V0 Constraints

Do NOT build yet:

* Payments
* Subscription billing
* Automated SMS
* Full AI voice calling
* Review marketplace
* Native apps
* Complex maps
* Automated pricing
* Full CRM
* Invoicing

Build the architecture so these can be added later.

The goal of V0 is to prove:

**Customer request → qualification → matching → tradie accepts → customer gets connected.**

Create a working prototype with realistic sample data where required, while clearly distinguishing demo data from real activity.
